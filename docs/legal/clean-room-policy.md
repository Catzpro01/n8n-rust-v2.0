# Clean-room development policy

The platform is independently implemented. Contributors may use public specifications and documentation, owner-created exports, and black-box behavior observations to write original requirements and conformance fixtures. They must not copy n8n source, enterprise-only files, UI assets, icons, product copy, or distinctive visual presentation into the clean-room repositories.

Compatibility names are used only to identify external formats or products and carry no endorsement claim. Third-party node packages are user-supplied compatibility inputs, installed separately under their own licenses, and never silently bundled. Provenance records identify the source of requirements and fixtures.

Before public or commercial release, counsel reviews the implementation, notices, compatibility claims, trademarks, and third-party inventory.

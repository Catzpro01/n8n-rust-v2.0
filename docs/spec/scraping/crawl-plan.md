# Crawl Plan

A Crawl Plan defines seeds, allowed and denied URL patterns, maximum depth and pages, canonicalization, refresh and deduplication, robots and rate policies, sessions and regions, authentication, extraction schemas, Artifact retention, adapter policy, and resource and money budgets.

Its URL Frontier is durable and provider-independent. Completion, retry, redirect, duplicate, blocked, skipped, policy-denied, and extraction-failed are distinct page outcomes. Resume after restart does not require a browser or provider job to remain in memory.
